Community issues we help resolve

Every day, wards across the district report the issues that matter most. DCRS routes them to the right officers and tracks them until they are fixed.

Utilities

Water supply disruptions

Broken mains, low pressure and outages leave neighbourhoods without clean water. Report them once — the district pipes the repair straight to the responsible unit.
